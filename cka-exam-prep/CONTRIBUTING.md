# Contributing

Thanks for your interest — but **this repository does not accept contributions**.

It's a personal CKA study log. The structure, wording, and choices reflect
how *I* learn, and merging external changes would dilute that.

## What you can do instead

- **Fork it** and make it yours (MIT license — no permission needed).
- **Star it** if it helped you. That's the most useful signal.

## Why no PRs?

- I want to keep authorship and responsibility for accuracy clearly on me.
- Reviewing PRs costs more focus than I want to spend during exam prep.
- Forks scale better than a shared canonical version for study notes.
